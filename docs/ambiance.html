Approved rules: Home and editor previews temporarily own only the lamps they affect, including cleanup/restoration; other ambiance lamps keep running. Multiple ambiance instances may share lamps, with a visible warning rather than automatic blending. Stopping an ambiance leaves its last state. Starting is explicit; no automatic playback after an app restart. Errors are visible and a failed mode is not marked running. Candlelight gains larger brightness variation and irregular timing with no more than one step every 600 ms. Halloween storm is a light-only effect with quiet gaps and occasional seconds-long bursts, not rapid strobing; gaps randomly use off or 1% warm white. On/off-only switches stay off between bursts. Sequence and target fields open scrollable choices; unavailable targets or missing timeline lamps disable Start. Running timelines cannot be saved or deleted until stopped, to preserve their audio files.
