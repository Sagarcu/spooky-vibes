3. Delete confirmation

Delete group?

Delete Living room? Your Hue lights are not deleted. Timelines using this group will need another group before playback.

Edit and Delete are icon-only buttons directly after the title, with accessible labels and 48px touch targets. No settings button, target controls, badge, or lamp count on group cards. Lamp names always appear under the title and wrap as needed. Editing and deletion are disabled during playback. The editor scrolls on small screens; wide screens keep the existing app layout. Save updates the same group; Back leaves it unchanged. At least one lamp is required. Unavailable saved lamps remain selected unless explicitly removed.
