Saved Ambiance

Phone-first Midnight Studio reference. Supersedes the earlier Ambiance proposal.

Approved flow: saved cards with Start/Stop, Edit and confirmed Delete. Creation saves a named effect, target, brightness limits, and RGB colors. Save does not start; Save & start does.
Approved addition: adjustable-white lamps get their own warm-to-cool setting, independent of RGB lamps. Storm has separate white-only lightning and glow settings. Keep current white is the default, preserving existing setups. The app reuses the Lights white-preset swatch menu alongside its slider and Keep current white action.
One shared white-only setting per saved mode, not per lamp. Fixed-white lamps keep their tone. Candlelight keeps plugs on; storm switches plugs off during every gap. Brightness limits remain shared.

Modes persist on-device without automatically starting. Stop and Stop all never delete saved setups. Editing a running mode asks to stop first. The Android background service can reconnect to modes still running.

Interactive reference only. No bridge requests. Demo changes here reset on reload. Use the tabs below the phone to inspect Home and Ambiance, and Add ambiance for the form.
