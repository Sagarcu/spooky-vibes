Menus and edge cases

  • Lamp menu: Move to room (including Unordered), Move lamp up/down. Alternatives to drag for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Room menu: Rename, Move room up/down, Delete with confirmation. Deletion unassigns its lamps; lighting groups remain unchanged.
  • Add/Rename: require a nonempty, case-insensitively unique room name; inline validation and visible save errors.
  • Keep empty rooms; hide empty Unordered except while dragging. Preserve unavailable lamps' assignments.
  • Persist before showing a successful move. Failed saves leave the previous arrangement intact and report the error.
  • Forget bridge removes local room organization along with groups and timelines. No Hue room or zone operations.
